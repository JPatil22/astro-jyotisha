How this is computed & verified

  • Planetary positions come from the astronomy-engine library (arc-second accurate), validated against NASA JPL Horizons to within ~0.001° — so Rashi, Nakshatra and pada are reliable even near cusps.
  • Sidereal chart uses the Lahiri ayanamsa. The Panchang matches Drik Panchang exactly in testing; Dasha and Ank Jyotish are computed exactly.
  • Doshas and Guna Milan follow standard whole-sign rules. These are a sound, standard reading — conventions genuinely vary between astrologers, so treat scores and dosha calls as guidance, not absolutes.
  • Interpretations (readings, gochar tones, remedies) are traditional guidance, not prediction. Jyotisha is for reflection, not decisions about health, finance, or law.

How to Capture the Perfect Palm Photo

Follow these 4 simple steps to ensure AI landmark tracking and sub-pixel crease detection are 100% accurate:

✅
1. Palm Facing Camera (Palmar Side)

Show your palm lines, NOT the back of your hand or fingernails.

✅
2. Open Palm Flat with Fingers Upward

Extend fingers naturally pointing up towards the top of the frame. Avoid clenched fists.

✅
3. Good Even Lighting

Use diffuse room light or daylight. Avoid intense glare or harsh deep shadows.

⚠️
4. If Image is Sideways?

Simply click the "🔄 Rotate 90°" button below the canvas to orient it correctly.
